# Market Snapshot (2026-04-15)

## Objective

Create a decision-oriented market baseline for GTM and pricing of Recrutaria.

## [KNOWN] Evidence from public sources

1. Workable publishes plan-based pricing publicly.
- Source: https://www.workable.com/pricing

2. Breezy HR publishes a self-serve pricing page with plan structure.
- Source: https://breezy.hr/pricing

3. Greenhouse positioning is enterprise-first and pricing is generally sales-led.
- Source: https://www.greenhouse.com/

4. Lever positioning is sales-led talent acquisition platform.
- Source: https://www.lever.co/

5. Gupy is a major ATS/recruitment platform in Brazil with enterprise positioning.
- Source: https://www.gupy.io/

## [INFERRED] Implications for Recrutaria

1. Transparent entry pricing creates lower friction for SMB acquisition.
2. Enterprise ATS competitors rely on sales process, leaving room for fast pre-paid onboarding.
3. Recrutaria can differentiate with:
- operational speed (WhatsApp + triage in one flow)
- pre-paid credits for low-risk entry
- optional recurring plan for predictability

## [SPECULATIVE] Hypotheses to validate in next 30 days

1. SMB recruiters convert better on a visible pre-paid starter package than demo-only funnel.
2. Candidate-facing pages can drive lower CAC if funnel includes immediate CV diagnosis CTA.
3. Recurring top-up plan may improve retention after first 30-45 days of active usage.

## Recommended GTM focus (next 90 days)

1. Primary ICP
- SMB and mid-market recruiting teams with high inbound candidate volume.

2. Offer structure
- Entry: pre-paid package
- Expansion: recurring monthly credit replenishment

3. Channel mix
- Paid search on high-intent terms (ATS, triagem curriculo, recrutamento whatsapp)
- outbound to small recruitment consultancies
- case-driven content from conversion pages (`/para-empresas`, `/precos`)

## Metrics to monitor weekly

1. Visit -> CTA click rate by page
2. CTA click -> register rate
3. Register -> first checkout created
4. Checkout created -> paid
5. Paid cohort repeat usage (30d)
